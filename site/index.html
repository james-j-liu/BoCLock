BoCLock — Bank of Canada Governing Council Hawkishness

An LLM pairwise tournament scoring Canadian monetary-policy communication on a hawkish–dovish spectrum: Governing Council members' speeches and parliamentary evidence alongside the Council's own rate announcements, Monetary Policy Reports, summaries of deliberations and press-conference statements. 0 = neutral; positive = more hawkish (tighter), negative = more dovish. Method adapted from FedLock; sister sites for the Bank of England and the ECB. Scores reflect LLM judgment, not official characterizations.
